Part 1: Team Charter

Team Name: The Fixers Pod

Team Lead: Esther

1. Communication Rules
Daily Sync: 15-minute stand-up via WhatsApp/Discord every day at 08:00 AM EAT.
Asynchronous Updates: All task movements on the board must happen on the same day the work is done. No batching on Friday.
Availability: Core working hours are 9 AM - 4 PM.

2. Deadlines & Commits
Commit Convention: <type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>. (Types: feat, fix, docs, style). No "wip" or "updated" messages allowed.
Code Freezes: All code for the MVP must be merged by Day 4 at 12:00 PM to allow for testing and audit logging.

3. Conflict Resolution & Escalation
Technical Disagreements: We will vote. In the event of a tie, Esther (Team Lead) has the final say.
The Anti-Black-Box Rule: No task can be larger than 4 hours. If it is, the assignee must break it down.
Escalation Path: If any team member has 0 visible activity (commits, messages, or board moves) for 1.5 days, Esther will reach out directly via phone. If unresolved by Day 2, the team will reassign their tasks and note it in the audit log.
